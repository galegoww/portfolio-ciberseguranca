# Créditos e produção

Título: DNS & Shield: O Ataque do Barão Pop-up.
Projeto de referência: DNS AI Shield, de Everton Macedo.
História e personagens originais desenvolvidos para este pedido. Animação de recortes 2D, com deslocamentos, rotações, barreira luminosa, janelas voando, legendas e vozes sintéticas em português. Trilha e efeitos sintetizados para este curta. Não é uma gravação do software em funcionamento.

As duas artes foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. O filme é uma fantasia e não demonstra a eficácia técnica do programa.

## Prompt de personagens

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent sprite atlas for a short ORIGINAL 2D animated cartoon about DNS and Shield protecting people from intrusive ads. Generate a wide horizontal transparent canvas divided conceptually into THREE EQUAL columns, no drawn grid. Each character is isolated completely within its own column with generous transparent margins, all full body, feet at same baseline. LEFT: DNS, a friendly youthful anthropomorphic CYAN glowing data-packet courier with small orange scarf, expressive eyes, sneakers, holding a tiny unlettered envelope, dynamic running pose facing right. CENTER: SHIELD, a brave friendly humanoid superhero shaped like a BLUE and SILVER shield with cyan luminous trim, short navy cape, arms and boots, powerful protective stance facing slightly right, confident smile, arm extended to block attacks. RIGHT: BARÃO POP-UP, a comical MAGENTA mischievous window-shaped villain, rectangular pop-up body with unlettered close X button at corner, expressive eyebrows, tiny top hat, orange gloves, pointed shoes, gleeful pose facing left. Clean thick ink outlines, polished cel shading, rounded readable shapes, vibrant modern Saturday morning 2D cartoon, original designs, charming not frightening. No other characters, no backdrop, no floor, no scene, no lettering or labels, no logo, no watermark. Genuine alpha transparency around the three sprites. All 3 silhouettes separate, never overlapping. Do not create a comic panel or storyboard.

## Prompt da cidade

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-screen 16:9 background plate for an ORIGINAL 2D animated cartoon about cyber heroes. A whimsical luminous digital city built from rounded browser-window buildings, cyan data roads, tiny floating envelope shapes in sky, soft violet skyline, cheerful blue twilight. Polished hand-drawn 2D cartoon with bold clean ink outlines and cel-shaded rich colors. Front wide street as a clean stage on bottom third, EMPTY with no people or characters. Buildings only behind the stage. Cinematic playful composition, city vanishing point in middle, readable uncluttered empty stage for moving characters to be composited later. Upper left and lower edge clear for titles and subtitles. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no UI labels. Landscape composition.
